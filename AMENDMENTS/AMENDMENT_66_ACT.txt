1990/Jyaishta 18, 1912 (Saka) The following Act of Parliament received the assent of the President
on the 7th June, 1990, and is hereby published for general information:— THE CONSTITUTION
(SIXTY-SIXTH AMENDMENT) ACT, 1990 [7th June, 1990.] An Act further to amend the Constitution of
India. Be it enacted by Parliament in the Forty-first Year of the Republic of India as follows:— 1.
This Act may be called the Constitution (Sixty-sixth Amend- ment) Act, 1990. 2. In the Ninth
Schedule to the Constitution, after entry 202 and before the Explanation, the following entries
