STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS
The concept of rulership, with privy purses and special privileges unrelated to any current functions and social purposes, is incompatible with
an egalitarian social order. Government have, therefore, decided to terminate the privy purses and privileges of the Rulers of former Indian States.
It is necessary for the purpose, apart from amending the relevant provisions of the Constitution, to insert a new article therein so as to terminate
expressly the recognition already granted to such Rulers and to abolish
privy purses and extinguish all rights, liabilities and obligations in respect
of privy purses. Hence this Bill.
