THE CONSTITUTION (THIRD AMENDMENT) ACT, 1954

THE CONSTITUTION (THIRD AMENDMENT) ACT, 1954.

[22nd February, 1955.]

An Act further to amend the Constitution of India.

BE it enacted by Parliament in the Fifth Year of the Republic of India as follows:-

1. Short title.-This Act may be called the Constitution (Third Amendment) Act 1954.

2. Amendment of the Seventh Schedule.-In the Seventh Schedule to the Constitution, for entry 33 of
List III, the following entry shall be substituted, namely:-

"33. Trade and commerce in, and the production, supply and distribution of,-

(a) the products of any industry where the control of such industry by the Union is declared by
Parliament by law to be expedient in the public interest, and imported goods of the same kind as
such products;

(b) foodstuffs, including edible oil seeds and oils;

(c) cattle fodder, including oilcakes and other concentrates;

(d) raw cotton whether ginned or unginned, and cotton seeds; and

(e) raw jute.".
