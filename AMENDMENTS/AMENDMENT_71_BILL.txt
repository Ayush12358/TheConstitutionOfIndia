THE CONSTITUTION (SEVENTY-EIGHTH AMENDMENT) BILL, 1992

A

BILL

further to amend the Constitution of India.

BE it enacted by Parliament in the Forty-third Year of the Republic of India as follows:-

1. This Act may be called the Constitution (Seventy-eighth Amendment) Act, 1992.

2. In the Eighth Schedule to the Constitution,—

(a) existing entry 7 shall be re-numbered as entry 8, and before entry 8 as so re-numbered, the
entry "7. Konkani." shall be inserted;

(b) existing entry 8 shall be re-numbered as entry 10, and before entry 10 as so re-numbered, the
entry "9. Manipuri." shall be inserted;

(c) existing entries 9 to 15 shall be re-numbered as entries 12 to 18 respectively, and before entry
12 as so re-numbered, the entry "11. Nepali." shall be inserted. THE CONSTITUTION (SEVENTY FIRST)
AMENDMENT BILL, 1992

A BILL FURTHER TO AMEND THE CONSTITUTION FOR THE PURPOSE OF INCLUSION OF CERTAIN LANGUAGES IN THE
EIGHTH SCHEDULE THERETO

ENACTED by Parliament in the Forty third Year of the Republic of India as follows :—

1. In the Eighth Schedule to the Constitution— (a) for entry 14, the following entry shall be
substituted, namely:— ‘14. Nepali’; (b) entry 21 shall be re-numbered as entry 24 and after entry
23, the following entries shall be inserted, namely:— ‘21. Konkani. 22. Manipuri.’;

STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

There have been demands for inclusion of certain languages in the Eighth Schedule to the
Constitution. It is proposed to include Konkani, Manipuri and Nepali languages in the Eighth
Schedule to the Constitution. The Bill seeks to give effect to this decision.

2. The Nepali language is also known in some areas as ‘Gorkha Bhasa’. In the Census operations,
other nomenclatures such as ‘Gorkhali’, ‘Gorkhi’, ‘Gurkhiya’, ‘Khaskura’ or ‘Naipali’ have also been
used.
