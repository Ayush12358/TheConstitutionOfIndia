LOK SABHA

The following Bills were introduced in Lok Sabha on 18th August, 2003:—

BILL No. 63 OF 2003

A Bill further to amend the Constitution of India.

BE it enacted by Parliament in the Fifty-fourth Year of the Republic of India as follows:—

1. This Act may be called the Constitution (One-Hundredth Amendment) Act, 2003. 2. In the Eighth
Schedule to the Constitution,— (a) existing entry 3 shall be re-numbered as entry 4, and before
entry 4 as so re-numbered, the entry “3. Bodo.” shall be inserted; THE GAZETTE OF INDIA
EXTRAORDINARY [PART II—

STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS

There have been demands for inclusion of certain languages in the Eighth Schedule to the
Constitution. It is proposed to include Bodo language in the Eighth Schedule to the Constitution.

2. The Bill seeks to achieve the above object.

NEW DELHI; The 7th August, 2003.
