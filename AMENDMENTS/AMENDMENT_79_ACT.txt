THE CONSTITUTION (SEVENTY-NINTH AMENDMENT) ACT, 1999

[21st January, 2000.]

An Act further to amend the Constitution of India.

BE it enacted by Parliament in the Fiftieth Year of the Republic of India as follows:—

1. (1) This Act may be called the Constitution (Seventy-ninth Amendment) Act, 1999.

(2) It shall come into force on the 25th day of January, 2000.

2. Amendment of article 334.-In article 334 of the Constitution, for the words "fifty years", the
words "sixty years" shall be substituted.

RAGHBIR SINGH,

Secy. to the Govt. of India.
