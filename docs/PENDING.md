# Pending constitution-amendment bills (as of 2026-10-03)

Bills that would become the 107th+ Amendment **if enacted** — tracked here so a
future enactment is a promotion (act PDF into `AMENDMENTS/`, row into
`docs/amendments.csv`, tag + history rebuild), not a fresh hunt. Nothing below
is enacted; all three PDF links were verified live (HTTP 200,
`application/pdf`, `%PDF` header) on 2026-10-03.

Constitutional amendments need a two-thirds special majority in each House
(and ratification by half the states where Part III / federal provisions are
touched), so "pending" routinely ends in JPC limbo or defeat — see the 131st.

## In committee

### 129th — simultaneous elections ("One Nation, One Election")

- **Bill:** The Constitution (One Hundred and Twenty-ninth Amendment) Bill, 2024
- **Introduced:** Lok Sabha, 17 Dec 2024 → Joint Parliamentary Committee, 19 Dec 2024 (Chair: P. P. Chaudhary)
- **Does:** inserts Art 82A; Lok Sabha + all Assemblies elected together from an
  "appointed date"; prematurely dissolved Houses serve only the remainder term;
  ECI may recommend deferring a state poll (no statutory threshold or time limit)
- **Companion:** The Union Territories Laws (Amendment) Bill, 2024
- **Status:** JPC extended 29 Jul 2026 — report due the first day of the last
  week of the Winter Session 2026; proposed 6 Oct 2026 SC interaction cancelled
  after Opposition/CJI objections (1 Oct 2026)
- **Bill text (PRS):** https://prsindia.org/files/bills_acts/bills_parliament/2024/Constitution_(One_Hundred_and_Twenty-Ninth_Amendment)_Bill,_2024.pdf
- **Tracker:** https://prsindia.org/billtrack/the-constitution-one-hundred-and-twenty-ninth-amendment-bill-2024

### 130th — removal of ministers upon detention

- **Bill:** The Constitution (One Hundred and Thirtieth Amendment) Bill, 2025
- **Introduced:** Lok Sabha, 20 Aug 2025 (Amit Shah) → Joint Parliamentary
  Committee, same day (Chair: Aparajita Sarangi)
- **Does:** amends Arts 75, 164, 239AA; a PM/CM/Minister accused of an offence
  punishable with 5+ years and detained 30 consecutive days is removed (PM/CM
  must resign by day 31, else deemed removed); reappointment after release
  possible. JPC draft (Jul 2026) softens "removal" to "suspension" + sunset clause
- **Companions:** The J&K Reorganisation (Amendment) Bill, 2025; The Government
  of Union Territories (Amendment) Bill, 2025
- **Status:** draft-report adoption deferred 17 Jul 2026 for wider stakeholder
  consultation; pending
- **Bill text (PRS):** https://prsindia.org/files/bills_acts/bills_parliament/2025/Constitution_(130th_Amendment)_Bill,2025.pdf
- **Tracker:** https://prsindia.org/billtrack/the-constitution-one-hundred-and-thirtieth-amendment-bill-2025
- **Analysis:** https://prsindia.org/files/bills_acts/bills_parliament/2025/Brief_Constitution_130th_Amendment_2025.pdf (PRS legislative brief; flags basic-structure concerns)

## Failed

### 131st — delimitation + women's quota on the 2011 census (negatived)

- **Bill:** The Constitution (One Hundred and Thirty-first Amendment) Bill, 2026
  (Bill No. 107 of 2026)
- **Introduced:** Lok Sabha, 16 Apr 2026 → **negatived 17 Apr 2026** (298 for,
  230 against; needed 352 of 528 present)
- **Would have:** raised the Lok Sabha cap 550 → 850; let Parliament pick the
  census for delimitation by ordinary law (2011 census in practice); started
  the 106th Amendment's women's reservation without waiting for Census 2027
- **Companions (both infructuous):** The Union Territories Laws (Amendment)
  Bill, 2026; The Delimitation Bill, 2026
- **Bill text (PRS):** https://prsindia.org/files/bills_acts/bills_parliament/2026/Constitution_(131st_Amendment)_Bill,2026.pdf
- **Tracker:** https://prsindia.org/billtrack/the-constitution-131st-amendment-bill-2026

## Watchlist (context, not bills)

- **Census 2027** (notified 16 Jun 2025): houselisting Apr–Sep 2026, population
  enumeration Feb 2027, reference 1 Mar 2027 (1 Oct 2026 snow-bound areas),
  caste enumerated. The 106th Amendment's reservation commences after
  delimitation on these figures — so no women's-reservation seats before the
  2029 election at the earliest.
- If the 129th passes first, its "appointed date" mechanics interact with the
  delimitation the 131st tried to force — track together.
